[market-apps]

The signed pacman repository the Omarchy Mobile Market installs from: apps built from the AUR and tested on a phone-sized screen, and the moarchy-apps Quickshell apps.

Nothing to set up: on Omarchy Mobile the Market installs from here after the phone's PIN, and its package adds this repository and trusts its key. (App Finder installs the same apps from the AUR.)
